# Custom Items – Maya Hat & Smaller Gear

## 1. Maya Crown (the “Maya Hat”)

**Suggested Item ID:** 19500 (or next free ID in your range)

| Property          | Value                          |
|-------------------|--------------------------------|
| Type              | Headgear (Upper)               |
| Defense           | 5–8                            |
| Slots             | 1                              |
| Required Level    | 50–70 (adjust)                 |
| Weight            | 50                             |
| Jobs              | All                            |
| Location          | Upper Head                     |
| Refineable        | Yes                            |

**Suggested Bonus (example):**
- +5 INT / +5 DEX
- +10% resistance to Insect race **or**
- Small chance to reflect magic (weaker than Maya Card) **or**
- +5% Max HP when in Ant Hell maps

**Description:**
> A majestic crown fashioned after the ant queen Maya.  
> It radiates the quiet authority of the colony.  
> Those who wear it are said to be recognized by the insects of Ant Hell.
